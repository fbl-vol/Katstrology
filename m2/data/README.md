Credit GeoNames when displaying a selected place. GeoNames supplies names, country/admin region, city-centre coordinates and an IANA timezone identifier, **not** verified individual birth coordinates or a certificate. `cities5000` covers cities above 5,000 population and administrative seats; it is not a complete gazetteer. Region codes may be incomplete. Place selection is local: neither queries nor dates are sent to GeoNames or another host. Public snapshot age, mislabelled locations and timezone-border ambiguity remain possible. Users can inspect/override the city-centre coordinates and timezone in Advanced; unknown places need explicit manual coordinates and timezone rather than a guessed match. Historical offsets come from the installed Python IANA tzdata/zoneinfo at calculation time, never from GeoNames' current offset columns.

This data is not Swiss Ephemeris data. Public distribution of the application and Swiss chart engine remain separately gated by `m2/NATAL-CHART.md` and `docs/PUBLICATION.md`.
