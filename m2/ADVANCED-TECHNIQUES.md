# Advanced calculation methods · local source contract

All methods use Kat's qualified Swiss Ephemeris 2.10.03 path, the pinned `sepl_18.se1` and `semo_18.se1` digests, tropical geocentric apparent ecliptic longitude, UTC birth instants converted to Julian UT1 with `utc_to_jd`, and `calc_ut` with checked `FLG_SWIEPH | FLG_SPEED`. Results name the method version and retain source chart IDs/revisions and data hashes. An exact supplied birth instant is not an independently verified birth time. The supported 1900–2099 UTC interval and explicit partial house/angle semantics remain. No old Live Horoscope version or GUI equivalence is asserted.

**Optional Chiron data:** Chiron is included only for a newly generated natal profile declaring `include_chiron: true`. Existing/default charts, archives and sky-only views keep their ten-planet set. Swiss `SE_CHIRON` uses the public main-asteroid `seas_18.se1` for 1800–2399 CE with Kat's narrower 1900–2099 guard. Astrodienst identifies the [file and coverage](https://www.astro.com/swisseph/swisseph.htm) and the [official GitHub data repository](https://www.astro.com/swisseph/swedownload_e.htm). Source snapshot `aloistr/swisseph` commit `94621aba9f3ff9c32cb721eac0380c30dbb153fe`, SHA-256 `a2cd8fc33807c78ca9a700c91c2e042258b12fc4796519e00781440b5ad8b2e2`, 223004 bytes. For owned offline preparation only, fetch `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aloistr/swisseph/94621aba9f3ff9c32cb721eac0380c30dbb153fe/ephe/seas_18.se1` and verify the hash before use; this does **not** install it into a running numerical cache. Missing/mismatched data or a non-Swiss fallback cannot produce an opted-in result. The separately [published 2024 asteroid table](https://cafeastrology.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/AsteroidsEphemeris2024.pdf) gives Chiron 18°16′ Aries, direct, March 20 at midnight New York (04:00 UTC), a minute-resolution reference only. No medical or trauma diagnosis is inferred from this symbolism.

- **Solar return v1:** **every** crossing of the selected natal Sun longitude in the half-open interval between successive *UTC Gregorian* anniversaries of the supplied birth instant, beginning with the requested anniversary year. This interval can contain zero, one or more crossings, including a legitimate next-calendar-year event; no crossing is discarded to force one result per selected year. February 29 in a non-leap year maps to February 28. A crossing at the first boundary belongs to this cycle; the end boundary belongs to the next. This may differ from a local-wall-time birthday or a solar return labelled by the calendar year of the crossing. The saved cycle bounds and count accompany the archived result/history; a zero-crossing interval is shown but not saved as a return. Geocentric roots are independent of the chosen reference coordinates; only house/angle geometry changes. The annual window is not exactly 365 elapsed days.
- **Lunar return v1:** every crossing of the selected natal Moon longitude in the supplied half-open UTC interval of at most 732 days, including one exactly at its start, up to 32 results; an interval with more than 32 reports incomplete coverage instead of discarding results. The chosen interval and count appear with supported results and their history. A lunar return is not the synodic phase or a calendar month.
- **Secondary planetary progression v1:** bracket the effective UTC instant between successive UTC Gregorian anniversaries of birth, with the same February 29 rule. Let n be the completed age in years and f the elapsed-second fraction between its two anniversary instants. Sample each planet directly at birth UTC + (n + f) * 86400 SI seconds, interpreted as the Swiss Julian UT1 input through the same UTC conversion. The planets are not interpolated from annual longitudes; speed, when displayed, is per symbolic ephemeris day, not lived day. Dates before birth, missing exact time and unsupported mapped ephemeris years are rejected, not treated as converse progression. Compare progressed planets with immutable natal degrees using the natal profile's aspect rules.
- **Progressed MC (calendar-Naibod-style) v1:** at birth UTC + n and n+1 elapsed SI days calculate astronomical ecliptic MC at the *birth* coordinates with Swiss houses. Interpolate forward across the circular 0° boundary using f; canonicalize to [0°,360°). No progressed ASC/houses are asserted. MCp contacts mean this interpolated MC relative to fixed natal planets. MCr Naibod contacts mean the directly sampled progressed planets relative to fixed natal astronomical MC; these are distinct from transiting planets relative to that same MC. Polar/failed natal or sampled MC is unavailable, without a house-system substitute. The separately selected MCp and MCr exact-contact timelines use half-open effective UTC intervals of at most 366 elapsed days; a 12-hour effective-date scan and ≤60-second root bracket are numerical resolution, not a prediction. A near-station MCr contact within 0.001° of a selected target returns an incomplete, unsaved result instead of claiming exhaustive coverage. The documented MCp bracket is not a directed or Kündig solar-key mode; solar arc is a separate explicitly labelled technique.
- **Four planetary returns v1:** selected saved natal Venus/Mars/Jupiter/Saturn longitude crossed by the **same actual geocentric planet**, tropical longitude, in a positive half-open UTC interval not preceding birth. At most 730/1460/5490/11712 days respectively. Scan once per day, split steps at a speed-sign-change station, refine every signed crossing to ≤1-second bracket and ≤0.00002° angular residual. Retain **all** direct and retrograde passes, including three-pass loops; never select a single pass as definitive. Near-station unresolved tangency fails as `partial_near_station_contact`, not a complete return set. Event cap 12 fails closed. Reference latitude/longitude change return-chart houses, not geocentric crossing time. Limit archived return-chart aspect rows to the first eight with explicit total/truncated metadata, retaining all planets and qualified houses. A return has no predictive outcome. Swiss-based anchors: a 2024-04-05 12:00 UTC natal Venus crosses its own longitude three times direct/retrograde/direct in 2025 Jan–May; Jupiter and Saturn later returns tested against independently sampled Swiss planet longitude, not a preselected anniversary formula.
- **Actual lunations/global eclipses v1:** geocentric apparent Moon−Sun ecliptic-longitude crossings at 0°/180° yield actual New/Full Moon *syzygy* instants (not local topocentric phase); independently call Swiss `sol_eclipse_when_glob`/`lun_eclipse_when` with `FLG_SWIEPH` for typed global eclipse UT maxima and convert returned UT1 JD with `jdut1_to_utc`. Sample Sun/Moon via checked Swiss-only position path at each instant; do not infer an eclipse from node distance. Preserve both syzygy instant and distinct eclipse maximum and link only the unique same-phase syzygy within two days; eclipse contacts sample **at maximum**, lunation contacts sample **at syzygy**. The half-open requested window is at most 732 days; six-hour syzygy scan/≤60-second root bracket are numerical resolution. A global eclipse is *not* a claim of local visibility. Target only a selected saved natal planet or qualified axis with a declared aspect/orb. NASA's [2024 eclipse catalog](https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/OH/OH2024.html) reports Apr 8 greatest solar eclipse 18:17 UT, Mar 25 penumbral lunar 07:13 UT and Sep 18 partial lunar 02:44 UT; minute-level independent checks do not establish exact engine equivalence. Swiss [programming reference](https://www.astro.com/swisseph/swephprg.htm) distinguishes global searches and UT timescales.
- **Progressed lunar phases v1:** on that same secondary UTC birth-to-effective calendar map, sample the directly progressed Moon and Sun and search exact signed Moon−Sun ecliptic separation of 0° (New), 90° (first quarter), 180° (Full) and 270° (last quarter). Count complete symbolic 360° cycles from birth before the requested window. The half-open effective UTC interval is at most 40×366 elapsed days; 5-day effective-date steps and ≤60-second bisection bracket are numerical resolution, not an event or timing prediction. Preserve both lived effective and mapped ephemeris UTC instants. This is not an actual-sky lunation/eclipse or a lunar return; no automatic life-stage verdict.
- **True solar arc v1:** the actual directly sampled secondary progressed Sun at the mapped UTC ephemeris instant (same Gregorian anniversary and February 29 policy as secondary progressions) minus the selected saved natal Sun, modulo 360°. In the supported 1900–2099 source/effective interval age is under 200 symbolic days, so a positive forward arc never completes a full solar revolution; modulo cannot mistake a hidden lap for a small arc. Apply this one arc uniformly to saved natal Sun–Pluto (and Chiron only when opted in), plus saved ASC/MC only when qualified. No new houses, planet velocities, retrograde state or simultaneous sky are asserted. Zero age produces zero arc; this is not a fixed 1°/year mean, converse, MCp or transiting Sun. Directed-to-natal aspects use the declared natal orb profile.
- **MC transit v1:** transiting planet longitude contacts the fixed natal *astronomical* MC, not whole-sign tenth cusp. Aspects follow the selected natal profile and report exact contacts, not in-orb influence or a verdict. The explicitly chosen UTC window is at most 732 days, start inclusive and end exclusive (an exact end contact belongs to the following window); hourly bounded scan and ≤60-second bisection are numerical resolution, not an observational claim. Unsupported natal houses/MC yield no MC contact rather than using a fabricated angle.
- **Directional synastry house overlays v1:** A’s ten saved natal planets (plus explicitly opted-in Chiron) are projected by ecliptic longitude at zero latitude into B’s own qualified saved house convention; reverse direction is calculated independently. Placidus uses recipient birth-time ARMC, latitude and obliquity, with the recomputed cusps checked against that immutable recipient’s saved cusps; whole-sign uses the saved recipient Ascendant’s sign. An exact cusp begins its own numbered house. Nodes and angles are not silently treated as planets. An unavailable recipient house set leaves only that direction unavailable, without substituting another system. Distinct selected owner/current chart versions, source hashes and engine/profile fences remain; no score is computed.
- **Composite v1:** shortest signed circular midpoint of corresponding ten natal planetary longitudes from two different, exact-time, compatible, owner-qualified natals; exactly opposite degrees have no unique midpoint and are marked ambiguous. Coincident positions and swapping people retain the same result. No physical instant, houses, angles, speed or retrograde claim. An ambiguous planet is omitted from the drawn ring and derived aspect list, while both natal degrees and the incomplete-point label stay in the saved table. Optional Chiron is included only if both source profiles explicitly opted in.
- **Uncorrected Davison v1:** arithmetic midpoint of the two UTC instants and **separate arithmetic means of their signed geographical latitudes and longitudes**. This uses Astrodienst's stated uncorrected convention, not its separate spherical/great-circle variant or corrected MC option. Signed longitude values near opposite sides of the date line can average close to Greenwich; the result declares this convention, rather than silently wrapping to a geodesic midpoint. Calculate natal-like geometry at the derived instant and synthetic coordinate, not as a new person's natal. Antipodal locations still have a coordinate average under this explicitly algebraic convention, but do *not* define a unique spherical midpoint; no geodesic result is claimed. Missing source coordinates/time makes the derived chart unavailable. The two natals must agree on their house and node conventions; a mixed pair is not silently resolved by choosing one person's profile. A qualified polar or failed derived-house calculation preserves planetary positions with no fabricated house set. Optional Chiron requires both source profiles to opt in. Source: [Astrodienst Chart Types](https://www.astro.com/cgi/h.cgi?f=gch&h=gch65&lang=e), “Davison Relationship Chart, uncorrected” and its separate spherical-midpoint description (accessed 2026-10-31).

Manual return forms default to the saved birthplace coordinates. An alternate place may be selected from the offline GeoNames city-centre catalogue (its ID must match the exact submitted coordinates), or entered as explicit coordinates. Archive records keep the reference coordinates and a bounded source label: saved birthplace coordinates, selected offline city centre, or user-supplied coordinates. An offline city is not a verified birth address. Changing place never changes the geocentric crossing. No location is inferred when the saved chart lacks one; changing a current natal does not rewrite an earlier saved return.

These methods do not claim numerical equivalence to other applications or Swiss data versions. Tests must independently check birthday brackets, mapping and angular geometry, and choose event-time tolerances with local angular speed; each root solver’s declared bracket is numerical resolution, not observational certainty. Derived results retain both source versions and are never promoted to an active natal. No manual calculation invokes a model automatically.
